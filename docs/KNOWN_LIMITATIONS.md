# Known Limitations & Boundary Conditions: Project Varsha

**Document Reference:** Varsha System Transparency & Limitations Register  
**Governing Rule:** N1 (Honest Provenance), N2 (No Fabricated Claims), N5 (No Gaming)  
**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## 1. Scientific & Meteorological Limitations

1. **Synthetic vs Real Skill Distinction (N1):** Results obtained in `synthetic` data mode demonstrate that the pipeline reliably recovers known non-linear structures and dynamical biases under controlled conditions. Synthetic verification metrics must **never** be cited as real-world atmospheric forecast skill.
2. **Convective Scale Precipitation:** Grid resolutions of $0.5^\circ$ ($\approx 55\text{ km}$) and $0.25^\circ$ ($\approx 27\text{ km}$) represent synoptic and meso-$\alpha$ scales. Highly localized micro-bursts, single-cell thunderstorms, and urban cloudbursts smaller than grid cell dimensions are not resolved and are represented as area-average probabilities.
3. **Winter Western Disturbances (WD):** The current post-processing formulation is explicitly tuned for the Indian Southwest Monsoon (JJAS). Winter precipitation associated with mid-latitude Western Disturbances over northern India is out of scope for the MVP and planned for future extensions.
4. **Extreme Sample Rarity ($\ge 204.5\text{ mm}$):** Extremely heavy rainfall events ($\ge 204.5\text{ mm/day}$) represent $<0.1\%$ of historical grid-cell days. While calibrated exceedance models are implemented, uncertainty bounds for this highest category are inherently wide and require conservative interpretation.

---

## 2. Geospatial & Administrative Limitations

1. **Sub-Grid District Fallback:** Districts with land areas smaller than the grid cell resolution ($\le 1$ cell centre) use a nearest-cell assignment fallback. These districts are explicitly badged as `sub-grid` in the user interface and data exports.
2. **Administrative Boundary Evolution:** District boundaries in India undergo periodic administrative reorganization (e.g. creation of new districts). Boundary datasets represent the base vector geometries documented in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`.

---

## 3. Operational & System Limitations

1. **Real-Mode Latency:** In `real` data mode, observational verification depends on the operational availability of IMD gridded products, which typically have a 24–48 hour consolidation latency.
2. **Indicative Alerts:** All district alert classifications (Green, Yellow, Orange, Red) generated by Varsha are automated statistical guidance outputs and do not constitute statutory or official IMD weather warnings.
